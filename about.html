About the project

Math is for people who ask “why?”

MathBridge is an independent, free mathematics education project focused on helping high school and early college learners explore ideas rather than memorize answers.

Our approach

A good explanation does more than present a formula. It shows where the idea comes from and invites you to experiment with it.

MathBridge combines short lessons, interactive tools, and self-check practice. The site is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and the learning resources are accessible without creating an account.

The project began as an independent student-built website. The goal is to keep improving the content through thoughtful feedback and, over time, make more mathematical ideas accessible.

What you can do here

  • Follow 16 guided lessons across number theory and probability
  • Explore six interactive math tools, including integer equations and the law of large numbers
  • Practice 48 original problems with answers, hints, and explanations
  • Track lesson and practice progress on this device
